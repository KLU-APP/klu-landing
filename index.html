How it works

Two minutes, twice a day

No app to learn, no journaling experience needed - just one clear question at a time.

01

A short voice note from Karolina, then one question to answer in your own words. A minute to hear, a little longer to sit with.
02

A quick mood check-in and one more question, a little deeper. Closes the loop before it follows you into tomorrow.

Why I built this

I used to spend way more time deciding than the decision itself deserved - and time is the one thing none of us gets back. Even after I'd finally decided, I rarely knew if it had actually been the right call.

That's why Klū exists - not something that decides for me, but something that helps me think it through, one piece at a time, instead of one thought crowding out everything else.

Once I stopped carrying every decision around in my head, I noticed something I didn't expect: clearer thinking made room for things I hadn't seen before - new ideas, new opportunities, and, honestly, more calm.

This 7-day practice is the smallest piece of that - one week to notice the same thing for yourself.

Karolina
Founder, Klū

Questions

Before you start

Is this therapy or medical treatment?
No. It isn't therapy or medical advice - it's a short structured practice for noticing and organizing overthinking, not a treatment for anxiety disorders. If you're in crisis, please reach out to a professional.
What if I miss a day?
Days stay unlocked in order from when you start. Miss one - pick up next time you open the page. No streak-shaming, no penalty.
Do I need the Klū app for this to work?
No. It works entirely on its own. Klū is there afterward, if you want the same kind of clarity every day - not just for one week.
