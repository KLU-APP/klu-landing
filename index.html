The problem

Deciding alone rarely feels clear.

Too many options

Every path looks reasonable until you're comparing all of them at once, in your head, at 1am.

You forget why you decided

Weeks later, the outcome is all that's left. The reasoning — the part that mattered — is gone.

How Klū helps

Structure for the moment you're stuck in.

01 — Too many options

You bring the question. Klū brings the clarity.

Describe the decision you can't get past, in your own words. Klū asks the follow-up questions you didn't think to ask yourself — until the situation actually makes sense.

02 — You forget why

A conclusion you can trust — and find again.

Klū lays out its reasoning, the main risk, and a next step — then saves the whole thread, so future-you can see exactly why past-you chose what they chose.

03 — Your mood matters

Mood, tracked alongside every choice.

A 30-second daily check-in. If you're deciding in an anxious or low-energy state, Klū notices — and says so, gently, in the conclusion.
